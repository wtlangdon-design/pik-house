Pik House Hub – Privacy

Pik House Hub is a private Home Assistant setup used by one household. It is not offered to the public.

When connected to Google Drive, it only creates and reads its own backup files in a "Home Assistant" folder in the owner's Drive. It cannot see any other files. Nothing is shared with anyone else, sold or used for advertising.

To disconnect it, remove "Pik House Hub" at myaccount.google.com/connections.
